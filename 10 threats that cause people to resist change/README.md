# 10 Threats That Cause People to Resist Change

Gap Selling is built on the idea that **all sales are about change** — and
that **customers don't like change**. Before a gap can be closed, the threats
that make a buyer cling to their current state have to be understood and
addressed. This is a working outline of those threats.

1. **Loss of control** — Change forces the buyer to hand over certainty about
   how things work today in exchange for an uncertain new process.

2. **Fear of the unknown** — People can evaluate the risk of a known problem
   more easily than the risk of an unfamiliar solution.

3. **Fear of failure** — If the change doesn't work, the buyer who championed
   it bears the consequences.

4. **Fear of looking bad / loss of face** — Adopting something new can imply
   the old way (which they may have chosen or built) was wrong.

5. **Increased workload** — Change usually means learning, migrating, or
   redoing work before any benefit shows up.

6. **Doubt about competence** — Buyers worry they or their team won't be able
   to execute the new way successfully.

7. **Ripple effects on other people or systems** — A change rarely stays
   contained; it can disrupt other teams, tools, or processes the buyer is
   responsible for.

8. **Past change fatigue** — A history of failed or painful changes makes
   buyers skeptical that this one will be different.

9. **Sunk cost in the status quo** — Time, money, and political capital
   already invested in the current approach make walking away from it feel
   like a loss.

10. **Genuine downside risk** — Sometimes the change really would leave the
    buyer (or someone specific) worse off, e.g. reduced headcount, budget, or
    influence — this is a real threat, not just a perceived one.

## Why this matters

Each threat above is a reason a buyer will resist even a change that closes
a real gap. Selling the size of the gap isn't enough — the threats that hold
the buyer in their current state have to be surfaced and neutralized, or the
deal stalls regardless of how compelling the future state is.
